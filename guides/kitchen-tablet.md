# Kitchen tablet

The board switches to a three-column kitchen layout on screens **960px wide or more**. A 10-inch tablet in landscape is wide enough; an 8-inch one shows the phone layout.

## What I use

**Amazon Fire HD 10**, bought on sale for about $73. It's a cheap 10-inch screen, and it's been plenty for one web page.

| Option | Approx. price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Amazon Fire HD 10 | $75–140 | Cheapest 10" screen. Uses Amazon's Silk browser instead of Chrome. |
| Samsung Galaxy Tab A9+ (11") | $150–220 | Regular Android with Chrome, so setup is easier. |
| Lenovo Tab M10/M11 | $150–200 | Similar to the Samsung. |
| Base iPad | ~$350 | Smoothest. Use Guided Access and set Auto-Lock to Never. |

Skip Echo Show and Nest Hub, since they won't run a custom page. Skip e-ink, since you can't tap ratings or add groceries on it. Avoid OLED screens on a display that's always on, because of burn-in.

## Fire tablet notes

- **Silk works.** Silk is based on Chromium, the same engine as Chrome. Sign in to Claude with the email code option instead of "Continue with Google."
- **Full screen without extra apps.** On wide screens the board has a **Full screen** button. It hides Silk's toolbar and asks the screen to stay on. You have to tap it again after the tablet sleeps.
- **Kiosk mode.** For a permanent setup, install **Fully Kiosk Browser**:
  1. In Silk, download the Android APK from fully-kiosk.com. Only use the official site.
  2. Open it. When Fire OS blocks the install, turn on **Allow from this source** for Silk, then install.
  3. In Fully, set the **Start URL** to your board link and sign in once using the email code.
  4. Turn on **Keep Screen On** and **reload on screen on**. That second one fixes the occasional blank white screen Fire tablets get.
  5. Optional: a nightly screen-off schedule, motion wake, and Kiosk Mode with an exit PIN. These are Plus features, about $8 once, and you can try them free with a watermark.

## Mounting (rental-friendly)

1. **Countertop stand** for the first week or two, while you find the right spot.
2. **Magnetic mount** on the side of a cabinet. It's removable and leaves no holes, and the tablet lifts off.
3. **Under-cabinet swing-down arm.** Keeps counters clear, and there's often an outlet right below.

For power, use an angled USB-C cable about 6 feet long, a paintable adhesive cord cover, and the charger that came with the tablet. Keep the tablet away from the stove, the sink, and direct sun. Because it's plugged in all the time, use a nightly sleep schedule to go easier on the battery.
